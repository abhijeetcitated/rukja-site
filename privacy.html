Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

RukJa is operated by Abhijeet Pratap Singh (shown on Google Play as RukJa). This page says what the RukJa app keeps, why, who processes it for us and how to delete it. The app shows a short version under Profile → How your data is used, with a link to this page.

In short

  1. RukJa is operated by Abhijeet Pratap Singh, Bhopal, India. Privacy contact: rukja.help@gmail.com (we reply within 24 h; formal grievances are answered within 90 days as required by the DPDP Rules).
  2. What we collect and why: your email, a scrambled password if you set one, and a user ID (to run your account); when you confirmed you're 18 or older (RukJa is for adults); if you sign in with Google, the name, profile picture link and Google account ID that Google shares (to run your account); your nickname, if you set one; your journal entries, reasons, if-then plan, two onboarding answers, urge and slip taps (to show your streak and personalise the coach); coach messages (to generate replies); whether 'Coach may read my journal' is on and when you accepted the coach notice (to follow your choices); how many coach messages and personal breathing-screen lines you used each day (for the free daily limit); crash reports (to fix the app); and basic sign-in records, like your internet (IP) address (to keep accounts safe). Nothing else.
  3. Who processes it for us: Supabase (database and login, Mumbai region), the AI provider via OpenRouter (coach replies — your journal lines are sent only if you switch 'Coach may read my journal' on), Resend (emails, once the app sends any), Sentry (crash reports), Google Play/RevenueCat (payments, once there is a paid plan). They act only on our instructions.
  4. You can withdraw consent at any time by switching the coach-journal toggle off or deleting your account — in the app (Profile → Delete account) or at https://abhijeetcitated.github.io/rukja-site/delete-account.html. Deletion removes your data from our systems; Google keeps your payment records under its own policy, and the delete page lists the few other records that are kept.
  5. Your rights: access, correction, erasure, nomination of another person, and a complaint to the Data Protection Board of India (DPDP Act 2023, ss. 11–14).
  6. RukJa is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly process a child's data; if you are under 18, do not create an account.
  7. If a data breach affects you we will tell you without delay — what happened, the likely impact, what we did, and whom to contact.
  8. We keep data only while your account exists or as the law requires. The records the delete page lists as kept are the only exception.

The details

On this phone

Your urge taps, slips, journal, reasons, plan and two answers are saved on this phone. Everything except the coach works without an account.

With an account

Your account keeps your email, a scrambled password if you set one, your nickname, when you confirmed you're 18 or older, and a backup of your urge taps, slips, journal, reasons, plan, answers and coach chat. Signing in with Google adds the basic profile Google shares, like your name, profile picture link and Google account ID. Our sign-in service also keeps basic sign-in records, like your internet (IP) address.

The AI coach

When you write to the coach, your message, your last 10 chat messages, your reasons, plan and two answers, your trigger tags and when you wrote journal entries, how many urges you logged, when your urges and your last slip happened, and how many you logged this week and last go to our server and to an AI model through OpenRouter, to write the reply. The first line of your 5 newest journal entries is included only if you switch 'Coach may read my journal' on. We ask OpenRouter for providers that do not collect your data.

The breathing screen

After you accept the coach notice, the breathing screen can ask the AI for one personal line, at most 10 a day, using your reasons, plan, answers, trigger tags and urge counts. It never uses your journal text.

Safety and ads

When a message talks about hurting yourself, the app answers with helplines itself and doesn't ask the AI. That check looks for common words and phrasings, so it can miss some. Nothing is sold or used for ads, and RukJa has no ads.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, a report goes to Sentry, our crash-report service: the error and where in the code it happened, the phone model, Android version, app version and a random ID for this install. It holds no journal or coach text and no screenshots, and it isn't linked to your account.

Deleting

You can delete journal entries and the whole coach chat in the app, and your whole account under Profile → Delete account: it goes from our systems and from this phone. The phone keeps only the account's ID, a random code, so nothing of the account can come back. You can also email rukja.help@gmail.com from your account's address, and we'll delete it within 7 days. The same on the web: https://abhijeetcitated.github.io/rukja-site/delete-account.html

Contact

Questions about your data: rukja.help@gmail.com. RukJa is for adults (18+).
