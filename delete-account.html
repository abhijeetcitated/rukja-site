Delete your RukJa account

RukJa is an app by Abhijeet Pratap Singh. You can delete your RukJa account and everything in it in two ways. You don't need to sign in to read this page, and you don't need the app to ask by email.

1. In the app

  1. Open RukJa and go to Profile (the person icon, bottom right). If you're signed out, sign in to that account first.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete my account.

The account is deleted at once, and the app signs you out. If your RukJa doesn't show Delete account, update it from Google Play, or use the email way below.

2. By email

Email rukja.help@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject "Delete my RukJa account". We first reply to that address to check the request really comes from you, then delete the account within 7 days of your request and tell you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email, your password (stored scrambled), your nickname, when you confirmed you're 18 or older, and the profile Google shares if you signed in with Google (name, profile picture link, Google account ID).
  • The backup of your urge taps, slips and journal entries.
  • Your reasons, plan and two onboarding answers.
  • Your coach chat and the coach's daily message counts.
  • When you delete in the app, the same data on that phone, including what you saved there before you signed in.

What is kept

  • Google Play keeps its own payment records under Google's policy. RukJa has no paid plan yet.
  • Crash reports in Sentry carry no account details, so there is nothing in them to link back to you.
  • Supabase, our sign-in service, keeps its own security logs of sign-in activity (for example the time, the email used and the internet address). Deleting the account doesn't remove those log lines.
  • The phone you deleted on keeps the account's ID, a random code, so nothing of the account can come back on it. Uninstalling RukJa, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes that too.
  • Another phone where you used the account, or every phone after a delete by email, keeps its copy of the account's data, hidden, until RukJa is uninstalled there or its storage is cleared.
